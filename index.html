04 / How I built the study

I treated public data as signals — not as internal truth.

I combined official corporate updates, Arc'teryx product and System 0 pages, public pricing, and recent customer discussions. Customer posts are directional evidence only; they are useful for finding questions, not for estimating prevalence.

01Official strategy

Growth priorities, DTC, Women’s, Footwear.

02Product system

Family, suffix, price and technical positioning.

03Customer signals

Recent purchase, sizing and repair discussions.

04Synthesis

Look for tensions supported by more than one source type.

05 / What I would do inside Arc'teryx

Turn the public signals into testable internal questions.

01Measure comparison friction

Repeated PDP visits, comparison events, site-search refinement and support contacts by family.

02Segment by experience level

Compare new vs returning customers to see where expertise changes product selection behavior.

03Connect fit to outcomes

Returns, exchanges, size-guide usage and conversion by product family and fit profile.

04Measure post-purchase value

Repair turnaround, repeat contacts, CSAT/NPS, trade-in behavior and future purchase after service interactions.
